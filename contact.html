Clear Communication

CONTACT & VISITATION FAQS

Answers to common questions regarding visits, tours, trial passes, and directions.

Can I drop by for a tour without booking in advance?

To preserve member privacy, training focus, and zero-wait arena flow, tours are conducted strictly by appointment with our Athletic Director. Appointments can be scheduled same-day via our concierge form or by phone.

What is included during the VIP facility walkthrough?

Your private 45-minute tour includes a full walkthrough of all 8 arenas, the biomechanical laboratory, cryotherapy recovery suites, and executive locker rooms, along with a complimentary guest day pass to experience our equipment.

How fast will the concierge respond to my message?

Our athletic desk responds to all digital contact inquiries within 4 business hours during operating times. For urgent tournament inquiries or lane reservations, please call our toll-free direct desk at +1 (800) 555-ELITE.

Where do I park when visiting Stackly?

Complimentary underground valet parking is provided for all prospective members and visitors at MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi, Salem. Simply pull up to the porte-cochère, and our valet team will park your vehicle securely.

Can I book arenas for private tournaments or media filming?

Yes. Our stadium court, floodlit FIFA pitch, turf cricket oval, and 50M pool are available for sanctioned tournaments, commercial sports filming, and private corporate athletic summits. Select "Media & Partnership" in the contact form above.
