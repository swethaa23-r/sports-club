THE 8 CERTIFIED SPORT COURSES

Every course at STACKLY pairs professional instruction, kinematic movement review, and tournament-standard arena access across 8 core sporting disciplines.

COURSE 01 • TEAM SPORT

Football Tactical & Striker Course

FIFA Quality Pro floodlit hybrid synthetic pitch. Master high-cadence passing cages, GPS movement tracking, transitional counter-pressing schemes, and competitive 7v7 and 11v11 match simulations.

Skill Level: All Levels (Beginner Academy to Pro Draft)
Training Availability: Mon – Sun: 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM • Morning & Evening Squads
COURSE 02 • TEAM SPORT

Cricket Batting & Bowling Telemetry Course

Six curated natural red-soil turf wickets complemented by indoor climate-controlled nets. Equipped with programmable automated bowling machines capable of 95+ MPH variable deliveries and stroke telemetry.

Skill Level: Foundational to First-Class Competitors
Training Availability: Tue – Sun: 06:30 AM – 09:30 PM • Weekend Match Leagues
COURSE 03 • TEAM SPORT

FIBA Basketball Hardwood & Shooting Lab

FIBA-regulation Robbins maple hardwood courts with impact-absorbing subfloors. Equipped with Spalding Arena hydraulic glass backboards, Dr. Dish All-Star shooting machines, and shot-arc telemetry.

Skill Level: Rookie Development to NCAA / Pro Showcase
Training Availability: Mon – Sat: 06:00 AM – 11:00 PM • Shooting Gun Slots
COURSE 04 • RACKET CLUB

Laykold Grand Slam Tennis Masterclass

Four US Open-grade cushioned Laykold indoor hardcourts. Outfitted with 1000-lux flicker-free LED tournament lighting, high-speed automated ball-tracking Hawkeye systems, and automated ball launcher suites.

Skill Level: Recreational Players to ATP / WTA Tour Aspirants
Training Availability: Daily: 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM • Floodlit Evening Slots
COURSE 05 • RACKET CLUB

BWF Badminton Smash & Reflex Course

Six dedicated BWF-sanctioned sprung wooden courts overlaid with anti-slip Yonex mats. Equipped with high-speed shuttlecock launchers, jump-smash velocity radar guns, and video analysis stations.

Skill Level: Club Enthusiasts to National Circuit Competitors
Training Availability: Mon – Sat: 06:00 AM – 10:30 PM • Sunday Club Open Play
COURSE 06 • SPEED & POWER

IAAF Track Sprint Velocity & Agility Course

400-meter 8-lane IAAF-certified Rekortan polyurethane full-pour track. Features laser timing gates, precision starting blocks, plyometric sand pits, and force plate telemetry for sprinting and jumping.

Skill Level: Youth Sprinters to Collegiate Track & Field Champions
Training Availability: Daily: 05:30 AM – 10:00 PM • Early Morning Speed Sessions
COURSE 07 • TEAM SPORT

Volleyball Vertical Spike & Sand Agility Course

Two indoor FIVB-specification shock-cushioned maple hardwood courts alongside two outdoor heated deep quartz-sand beach volleyball courts with electronic scoreboard scoring.

Skill Level: Casual Rec Leagues to Semi-Pro Beach & Indoor Teams
Training Availability: Mon – Sun: 07:00 AM – 10:00 PM • Beach Sunset Ladders
COURSE 08 • AQUATICS

Olympic Natatorium Cadence & VO2 Swim Course

Olympic 50-meter 10-lane competition pool with movable bulkhead. Features Omega timing touchpads, underwater 4K stroke biomechanics cameras, UV and ozone micro-filtration, and 26.5°C constant temperature.

Skill Level: Stroke Refinement to Masters & Olympic Trials
Training Availability: Daily: 05:30 AM – 10:00 PM • Dedicated Lap & Coach Slots
