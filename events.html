Competition Standards

REGISTRATION GUIDELINES & CRITERIA

Everything team captains and individual athletes need to know prior to submitting competition entries.

01

Eligibility & Roster Checks

All players must submit government or federation ID verification. Rosters lock strictly 48 hours prior to tournament draw day.

02

Certified Officials & Telemetry

Every match is officiated by certified federation referees. High-speed video review (VAR/Hawkeye) is available for key decisions.

03

Entry Fees & Inclusions

Entry fee includes refereeing, official match balls, medical paramedic standby, hydration station access, and 4K match video archives.

04

Prize Money & Accolades

Purse payouts are wired within 48 hours post-championship. Winners receive handcrafted Stackly gold trophies and individual medals.
