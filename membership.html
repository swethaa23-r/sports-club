Answers & Policies

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear guidelines on membership freezes, capacity rules, guest passes, and cancellation terms.

How does the 600-member capacity cap work?

To guarantee zero wait times for Olympic platforms, laykold courts, and swim lanes, active memberships across the entire complex are capped at strictly 600 athletes. Once the cap is reached, new applications are placed into a chronological waitlist.

Can I freeze or pause my membership when traveling?

Yes. Standard, Premium, and Elite tier members can freeze their membership up to 60 days per calendar year with zero pause fees. Simply submit a notice 5 business days in advance via our mobile app or your dedicated concierge.

Can I bring training partners or guests?

Standard members receive 1 guest pass per month. Premium members receive 2 guest passes per month. Elite VIP members enjoy unlimited accompanied guest privileges (up to 2 guests per visit). Additional guest passes can be booked at $45 per day.

Are masterclass reservations guaranteed?

Masterclass bookings open 7 days in advance at 06:00 AM on the Stackly app. Premium members can book up to 4 classes ahead of time, while Elite members enjoy priority front-row reservations up to 14 days in advance.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Monthly memberships can be cancelled at any time with a 30-day written notice. Annual memberships offer our maximum 20% discount rate and are committed for 12 months, with transferability to family members or colleagues if you relocate.

When is payment collected?

No payment is charged upon online application submission. Your membership begins only after concierge review, athletic goal alignment, and physical keycard issuance during your personal arena orientation.
