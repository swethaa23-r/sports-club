Four Progression Tiers

TRAINING LEVELS

Every athlete enters through an initial biometric intake assessment to place them into the optimal progression pathway.

LEVEL 01 • FOUNDATION

Beginner Training

Focuses on gross motor coordination, fundamental movement patterns, sport rules, and safe mechanics.

Target: Youth & New Athletes
Frequency: 2 – 3 Days / Week
Coach Ratio: 1 : 8 Max
  • Foundational kinetic footwork & balance
  • Basic ball handling & racquet swing path
  • Core aerobic endurance & posture control
  • Supervised non-contact scrimmage games
LEVEL 02 • DEVELOPMENT

Intermediate Training

Develops tactical execution, high-cadence drills, competitive decision-making, and strength conditioning.

Target: Club & High School Athletes
Frequency: 3 – 4 Days / Week
Coach Ratio: 1 : 6 Max
  • High-speed passing & shot velocity drills
  • Progressive barbell strength & sled sprint power
  • Weekly sanctioned inter-club league matches
  • Video stroke/stride breakdown with coach
LEVEL 03 • COMPETITIVE

Advanced Training

Rigorous tournament preparation, collegiate showcase exposure, and neuromuscular velocity optimization.

Target: State & Collegiate Contenders
Frequency: 4 – 5 Days / Week
Coach Ratio: 1 : 4 Max
  • Championship tactical systems & scouting reads
  • Velocity-based training & force plate audits
  • Collegiate scouting camp invitations
  • Cryotherapy & contrast hydro plunge suite
Elite Mastery

Scientific Protocol

THE 5-PHASE TRAINING METHODOLOGY

Every session at STACKLY follows a tested physiological pipeline designed to maximize neuromuscular gains while preventing central nervous system overtraining.

01

Dynamic Warm-Up

Neuromuscular priming, active joint capsule mobility, multi-planar tissue activation, and core temperature elevation to prime the kinetic chain for heavy output.

02

Skill Development

High-cadence sport-specific technical drills executed under zero fatigue: rotational racquet paths, passing vision, bat velocity, and jump mechanics.

03

Strength & Conditioning

Velocity-based power cycles with Eleiko barbells, Keiser pneumatic resistance, sprint sled drives, and explosive rate-of-force development.

04

Performance Evaluation

Dual force plate telemetry, wireless laser timing gates, heart-rate variability mapping, and 4K kinematics stroke replay to audit daily progress.

05

Recovery

Sub-zero -110°C Mecotec whole-body cryotherapy, 42°F contrast mineral plunge pools, infrared saunas, and pneumatic Normatec compression lounges.
